Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. What data we collect

When you create an account we collect your email address and (optionally) phone number, and the biodata you enter in your profile (name, age, city, education, profession and similar details) and the photos you upload. Payment details are processed by Razorpay; we never store your card or bank details.

2. How we use it

Your data is used only to run the Bandhan matrimonial service: showing your profile to other members according to your package, letting members send interests, and sending you account emails (confirmation, password and subscription reminders).

3. Who can see your profile

Your photos and full biodata are visible only as per your package rules — free members see blurred photos and limited biodata. Your contact details (email/phone) are visible only to members on a package that includes contacts. We never sell your data.

4. Verification documents

If you submit a government ID for verification, we store only the last 4 digits of the document number and your verification status. The full document number is never stored.

5. Your rights (DPDP Act, India)

You can export all your data from your account (GET /api/me/export) and delete your account and all associated data at any time (DELETE /api/me with confirmation). Deletion is permanent. You can also ask us to correct your data by updating your profile.

6. Data security

Passwords are stored only as bcrypt hashes. Photos are stored with a provider (Cloudinary/Supabase) and served through short-lived signed links. Access is logged for security (audit logs).

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email us at the support address shown on the Bandhan website.
